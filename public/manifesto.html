Origin

TradeSimple began during finals week. A friend and I kept asking the same question: why does everyone wait for the headline when the record already exists?

Congress.gov. USASpending. Lobbying disclosures. Campaign finance. None of it was hidden. It was scattered.

We named it TradeSimple because the job was assembly: make the path from government action to market exposure legible in plain English.

The wrong turn

Politician portfolios are a clue. They are often a late one.

The 45-day disclosure window means the trade may be done before the filing surfaces. That is not an attack on disclosure tools. It is a timing problem. The signal sits closer to votes, contracts, and money in motion.

The better signal

Follow the vote, the spend, and the influence.

Vote

Committee markup, floor passage, authorization language that reprices sectors.

Spend

Contract awards, amounts, agencies, timelines, even when updates land late.

Influence

Lobbying pressure, PAC maps, seats that could change the business environment.

Bill advances → agency language → USASpending and LDA crosswalks → exposed tickers on your watchlist, with confidence and source labels. Not a headline. A path.

The scale

The institution writing the rules is also one of the largest allocators of capital on Earth.

Treat public policy the way you treat any other input to exposure. Source it, timestamp it, map it to symbols, test the thesis in paper mode before you risk capital.
